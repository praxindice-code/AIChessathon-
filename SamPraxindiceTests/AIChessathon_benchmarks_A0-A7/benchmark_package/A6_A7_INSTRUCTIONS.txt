A6 AND A7 -- IMPLEMENTED CUMULATIVE SEARCH VARIANTS

Copy your chosen complete engine into engines/A6 and engines/A7, then replace
only search.py with variants/A6/search.py or variants/A7/search.py respectively.
Your supplied agent.py already supplies soft/hard deadlines, so NO agent.py
change is required. Keep matching NNUE loaders and weights in each copy.

A6 = A5 + score-drop time extension
After a completed iteration at depth >=5, compare with the preceding completed
iteration. A non-mate score drop >=80cp OR a best-move change permits one third
of the gap from the original soft deadline to the hard deadline. These triggers
do not stack. Hard deadline never changes. Interrupted iterations cannot trigger
the extension. Scores reset on every think(), including pondering.

A7 = A6 + selective quiescence checks
Each entry from negamax/razoring gets one optional quiet check per path.
Captures/promotions preserve this budget; elective quiet checks consume it.
In-check nodes search all legal evasions regardless of budget. Mandatory
counterchecking evasions do not consume the elective-check budget.
Quiet checks bypass capture pruning. Promotions also bypass capture SEE/delta
pruning in this variant. The previous whole-node delta shortcut is removed
so it cannot hide quiet checks. Bad captures are skipped individually.
All moves are generated and legal-move existence is checked before stand pat,
so stalemate scores zero and mate takes precedence over a draw counter.
Quiescence checks threefold, the halfmove clock and insufficient material.
MAXPLY and existing node-clock checks bound extended checking sequences.
The MAXPLY evaluation fallback and inherited negamax draw ordering remain
unchanged. This is not a complete rules/search correctness overhaul.
Extra move generation and legality checks may reduce NPS; benchmark strength.
